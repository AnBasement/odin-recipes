Lemon Risotto

Lemon risotto is a fresh dish, and can be served on its own or with prosciutto!

Ingredients

  • 9 dl chicken broth
  • 2 dl water
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 5 dl arborio rice
  • 2 dl dry white wine
  • 1 dl grated parmesan
  • 1 tbsp lemon zest
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • chopped parsley

Steps

  1. Heat broth, water and salt to a simmer and reduce the heat to low to keep it warm.
  2. Heat butter and oil in a dutch oven. Stir in rice till it's all coated. Keep stirring till all rice kernels seem translucent on the edges.
  3. Add in the wine and keep heat on for a few more minutes, stirring constantly, untill all wine is absorbed.
  4. Reduce heat to low. Continue stirring while adding broth, a ladle at a time. Stir until most of th ebroth is absorbed between each ladle.
  5. When rice is al dente, stir in the lemon zest, juice and parmesan. Remove from heat and garnish with chopped parsley. Serve with proscuitto.
