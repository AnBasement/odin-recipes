Pulled Pork

Pulled pork can be eaten by itself, in tacos, as a burger or however you like!

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg pork neck
  • salt
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tbsp crushed cumin
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 pinch cinnamon
  • 2-3 tbsp chipotle paste or similar
  • 5 dl pineapple juice
  • neutral cooking oil

Steps

  1. Slice the meat into evenly sized pieces. Season and brown on all sides in some oil.
  2. Add chopped garlic, cumin, oregano, cinnamon, chipotle paste and pineapple juice. The liquid should cover the meat about halfway.
  3. Put the lid on the pressure pot and set it to high for about 1 hour.
  4. Once finished, release pressure. Remove and pull the meat. Thicken the sauce and adjust flavoring if necessary before adding meat back in.
