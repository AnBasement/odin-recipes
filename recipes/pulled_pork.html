Pulled Pork

Pulled pork can be eaten by itself, in tacos, as a burger or however you like!

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg pork neck
  • salt
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tbsp crushed cumin
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 pinch cinnamon
  • 2-3 tbsp chipotle paste or similar
  • 5 dl pineapple juice
  • neutral cooking oil
